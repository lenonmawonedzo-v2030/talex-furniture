Who We Are

Talex Royal Furnitures is a Harare furniture business specialising in office and business furniture: executive desks, L-shape desks and reception counters, built to give your office a professional first impression.

Talex Royal Furnitures started with a simple belief: every office, shop and business deserves furniture that looks professional and lasts. What began as a small Harare workshop making stools and chairs has grown into a trusted name for office desks, reception counters and custom-made business furniture.

Every piece we make is built with durable materials and finished with care, because the furniture in your office is often the first thing your clients notice. Whether you need a single desk or a full reception area, we work with you to get the design, size and finish right, at a price that makes sense.

Visit us at Southerton Shops, opposite Delta, or send us a message on WhatsApp. We'd be glad to help you create a space that makes the right first impression.

What We Offer

  • L-shape and executive office desks
  • Reception counters and front desks
  • Salon and shop counters
  • Modern LED reception units
  • Custom designs in various sizes
